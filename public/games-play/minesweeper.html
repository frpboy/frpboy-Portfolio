How to play

Play Minesweeper free online. Tap a cell to reveal it. Numbers tell you how many mines touch that cell. Avoid all mines and reveal every safe cell to win. Right-click, long-press, or turn on Flag Mode and tap to place a flag where you think a mine is. Your first click is always safe. No download needed, works on mobile and desktop.
