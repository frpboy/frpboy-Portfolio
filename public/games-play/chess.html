How to play

Play Chess free online against the computer. You are White. Tap a piece to select it, green dots show its legal moves, then tap a highlighted square to move. Pawns promote to a queen automatically. Castling and en passant are supported. Checkmate your opponent king to win. No download needed, works on mobile and desktop.
