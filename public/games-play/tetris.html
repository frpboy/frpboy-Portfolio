How to play

Play Tetris free online. Falling blocks stack on a 10 by 20 board. Move with the left and right arrow keys, press up to rotate, down to move faster, and space for a hard drop. Fill a whole row to clear it and score. The game speeds up as your level rises. Use the on-screen buttons on mobile. No download needed, works on mobile and desktop.
