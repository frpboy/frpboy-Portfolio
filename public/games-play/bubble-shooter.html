Bubble Shooter

Aim with the mouse or your finger, click or tap to shoot. Match 3 or more of a colour to pop them.

How to play: Bubble Shooter is free to play online with no download, on mobile and desktop. Move the mouse or drag on the screen to aim the shooter at the bottom, then click or tap to fire the bubble. Match 3 or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them, and bubbles left hanging drop for bonus points. Every 5 shots that do not pop anything, a new row pushes in from the top. If the bubbles cross the line above the shooter, the game is over. Clear the whole board to win.
