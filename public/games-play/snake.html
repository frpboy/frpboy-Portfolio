How to play Snake

Tap Start and steer the snake with the on-screen pad, by swiping, or with the arrow keys and WASD. Eat the green dots to grow and score 10 points each - but do not hit the walls or your own tail. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard to change the speed. Free to play, no download and no sign-up.
